Frequently asked

Questions worth asking.

What can we hire Solas to do?

Our three primary services are AI and technology consulting, custom software and AI solutions, and product, project and program leadership. We work across industries and shape engagements around the business problem.

Do projects have to involve private AI?

No. We build business software, automate workflows, integrate systems, and lead technology programs. Private and sovereign AI is a specialist capability we bring where it fits the project.

What is sovereign AI?

Sovereign AI is about retaining meaningful control over your AI capability: data, models, infrastructure, governance, and operating choices. The right design depends on your environment and obligations.

Can local and cloud AI coexist?

Yes. A hybrid approach can keep sensitive workloads within your controlled environment while using cloud services for suitable tasks. Data flows and access boundaries should be explicit.

Do we need dedicated hardware?

Not always. The answer depends on model size, workloads, performance expectations, and existing infrastructure. Assessment helps determine an appropriate starting point.

Can you help before we know what to build?

Yes. AI readiness assessments, strategy work, and executive workshops are designed to clarify opportunities and constraints before you commit to implementation.

How long does an engagement take?

It depends on scope, infrastructure, integrations, and governance requirements. We agree priorities, deliverables, and a realistic timeline before the engagement begins.

Is the Sovereign AI Appliance available?

The appliance is in development and is not yet for sale. Consulting, custom software and AI solutions, and delivery leadership are available today. The appliance remains a research and development project.

Are you looking for a technical co-founder?

Solas welcomes conversations with potential technical co-founders, architects, engineers, and design partners who are interested in building the platform. Mention your experience and interests in your email.
