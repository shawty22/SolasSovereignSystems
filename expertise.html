AI Resilience for Critical Operations

Can your team isolate an AI system and keep an essential service running safely?

Discuss an assessment →

Solas is scoping an assessment for critical-infrastructure operators and industrial controls integrators. Start with one service and map where local or cloud AI could affect it, who has authority to intervene, and what the site needs to operate independently.

  • Dependency and authority map

    Document model and network dependencies, paths to equipment controls, and who can authorize isolation and recovery.

  • Proposed isolation and recovery exercise

    Define a tabletop or isolated lab exercise, the evidence to collect, and the criteria for safe operation and return to service.

  • Prioritized remediation plan

    Identify gaps, assign proposed owners, and define how to verify each change.
