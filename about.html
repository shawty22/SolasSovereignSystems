Meet the founder

Ciarán O’Hara

An Irish-born technology leader and product strategist with more than two decades of experience in Silicon Valley.

Ciarán’s work spans enterprise infrastructure, product development, privacy, and complex technology programs. His experience includes privacy initiatives at Meta and cryptographic services at Apple, alongside work with Yahoo!, Symantec, Pearson Education, and Marvell Technology.

He combines technical programme leadership with product strategy and human-centered design. A certified Project Management Professional, he holds a bachelor’s degree in Business and Information Communication Technology from Ireland and completed Product Management certification through Stanford Continuing Studies in 2020.

At Solas, that experience informs a practical focus: helping organizations make deliberate decisions about AI, protecting sensitive knowledge, and building systems their own teams can understand and operate.

Technology with a wider purpose.

Ciarán’s commitment to conservation grew through work connecting ARC Amazon with Silicon Valley. In 2018, he conceived and led a documentary expedition along Peru’s Las Piedras River headwaters, bringing together filmmakers, conservationists, and local communities.

He completed Climate Reality Leadership Corps training in 2019 and later graduated from the Climatebase Fellowship. In 2026, he founded the Global Local AI Foundation, bringing together his interests in privacy, sustainability, and locally controlled technology.

He also serves as Chief Product Officer at Sophurion, contributing to AI-powered predictive risk intelligence.

Research & practice

A shared interest.
Separate organizations.

Global Local AI Foundation researches the choices shaping AI. Solas helps organizations put practical systems to work.

The foundation is an independent U.S. 501(c)(3) nonprofit. Solas is a separate, for-profit consulting and technology business. The organizations share a founder and an interest in responsible, locally controlled AI; they are distinct entities.

The foundation’s research library is a useful starting point for questions around AI infrastructure, governance, privacy, and sustainability.
