Meet the founder

Ciarán O’Hara

A technology leader and product strategist with more than two decades of experience in Silicon Valley.

Ciarán’s work spans enterprise infrastructure, product development, privacy, and complex technology programs. His experience includes privacy initiatives at Meta and cryptographic services at Apple, alongside work with Yahoo!, Symantec, Pearson Education, and Marvell Technology.

He combines product strategy and human-centered design with the coordination that complex delivery requires: clear priorities, technical teams, vendors, and stakeholders working toward a shared outcome.

A certified Project Management Professional, Ciarán holds a bachelor’s degree in Business and Information Communication Technology from Ireland and completed Product Management certification through Stanford Continuing Studies in 2020.

At Solas, he applies that experience to technology advice, software and AI solutions, and delivery leadership shaped around each client’s business.
